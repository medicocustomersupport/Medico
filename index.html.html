About Medico

Home healthcare made simpler

Medico is a home-healthcare booking platform focused on scheduled, non-emergency care and support at home. The focus is a clear service request process, professional onboarding, transparent starting prices and verified post-service feedback.

Our operating principles

  • Non-emergency home-care requests only
  • Clinical suitability and professional availability matter
  • Professional credentials reviewed before activation
  • Patient information handled with care and privacy
  • Clear pricing confirmation before service

Frequently asked questions

Important information before booking.

Are the displayed prices final?

The displayed attendant-care shift prices, Doctor Home Visit price and Physiotherapy price are shown as Medico’s listed service prices. Additional consumables, equipment, procedures or clinically required add-ons, if any, should be confirmed before payment.

Can Medico treat emergencies at home?

No. For severe symptoms or a life-threatening emergency, seek immediate emergency care.

Can I use my current location?

Yes. Tap “Use Current Location” and allow browser permission. Still enter the complete address and landmark.

Will every requested procedure be performed?

Only when clinically appropriate and within the professional's scope. Some procedures may require a valid prescription or prior assessment.

Are services available 24 hours?

Medico accepts service requests 24×7. Actual visit timing depends on clinical suitability, location and professional availability. This does not replace ambulance or emergency services.

How are healthcare professionals verified?

Professional applications are reviewed for identity, qualification/training and, for nurses, applicable nursing-registration credentials before profile activation.

How do I track my booking?

After submission, Medico generates a Booking ID. Enter that ID and the registered mobile number in “Track Your Medico Booking”. On this static version, tracking works on the same browser/device; cross-device tracking requires Medico's booking backend.

Is mobile OTP verification active?

The website includes an OTP-ready verification flow. Real SMS OTP becomes active after Medico connects a secure OTP provider/backend; GitHub Pages alone cannot securely send or verify SMS OTP.

Can I rate the nurse after my service?

Yes. The rating flow is designed for completed nursing services using the booking reference and registered mobile number, with one verified review per completed booking.

How can a nurse or caretaker register?

Choose “Nurse / Caretaker” near the top of this page. Complete the professional registration form and upload the required documents. The profile remains pending until Medico admin verification and approval.

Can I cancel or reschedule a confirmed booking?

Yes. Free cancellation or rescheduling is generally available when requested at least 4 hours before the confirmed visit. Late cancellations, professional dispatch, specially arranged consumables or a customer no-show may attract charges. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy for details.

How long do approved refunds take?

Medico aims to initiate approved refunds promptly. After initiation, banks and payment providers may take approximately 5–10 business days to credit the amount to the original payment method.
